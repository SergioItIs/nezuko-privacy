Privacy Policy

Your information, your collection, your choices.

For privacy questions or to request deletion of your data, email nezukoanimesocial@gmail.com.

1. About this policy

Nezuko Bot (“Nezuko,” “we,” “us”) is a Discord entertainment and social gaming application operated by the Nezuko Bot team. This policy explains how we handle information when you use Nezuko’s card collections, missions, games, profiles, social features, and support services.

For privacy questions or requests, contact nezukoanimesocial@gmail.com. This policy is effective from 1 October 2026.

2. Information we process

The information processed depends on the commands you use, the features enabled in your server, and the access Discord provides:

  • Discord user IDs and available profile information, such as usernames, display names, and avatars, to identify your game account and display interactions and profiles.
  • Server and channel IDs, member roles and membership updates, relevant permissions, and configuration settings, to deliver welcome messages, handle joins and departures, and apply automatic roles and level- or activity-based role rewards.
  • Gameplay records, including collected cards, Moon Fragments (MON), character relationships and affection, missions, levels, rewards, profile customizations, and competition results.
  • Command and interaction inputs, including slash commands, supported n. text commands, quiz answers, activity-event responses, selected options, and users you choose to interact with. Message text is processed transiently and is not stored outside Discord.
  • Presence and streaming-activity updates, processed transiently to grant streaming roles where enabled, without storing this activity data outside Discord.
  • Support communications and information you provide with reports, including your email address if you contact us by email.
  • Operational information needed to diagnose errors, enforce cooldowns, prevent abuse, and maintain the service, such as event timestamps, command outcomes, and error details.

We use this information to provide requested features, support progress across sessions and servers within the retention limits below, manage community roles, respond to support requests, maintain reliability, and protect the game and its users. Do not submit passwords, payment credentials, or sensitive personal information in commands or support reports.

3. Discord gateway intents

Nezuko’s access is limited by Discord’s API, approved intents, server permissions, and enabled features. Processing information to respond to an event does not necessarily mean storing it.

Server Members access supports welcome messages, automatic role assignment, role rewards, and handling members joining or leaving a server. Member identifiers, relevant profile information, roles, and membership updates are used to identify members and apply community settings, including roles awarded for levels and activity.

Presence access is used to detect when a member is streaming and automatically grant a streaming role in servers where this feature is enabled. Presence and streaming-activity information is processed transiently for this feature and is not stored outside Discord. Users can opt out of presence tracking.

Message Content access allows Nezuko to recognize and interpret n. prefix commands, such as n.hug and n.pat, alongside slash commands. It also supports quizzes and activity-based events in which users participate by typing answers or responses in chat. Message content is processed to handle these interactions and is not stored outside Discord. Users can opt out of message-content tracking.

We do not use message content to train machine-learning or AI models. Discord data is not sold or scraped to build unrelated datasets. For assistance with either opt-out, contact nezukoanimesocial@gmail.com.

4. Visibility and sharing

Bot responses in a Discord channel are visible to people who can access that channel. Profiles, showcases, social interactions, and competition results can reveal your displayed identity and gameplay information. Because Nezuko supports cross-server progression, your game account and collection are not confined to a single server.

We limit access to personal information to team members and service providers who need it to operate, host, maintain, secure, or support Nezuko. We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to a valid legal request, protect users, or investigate misuse. If the service changes ownership, any transfer of personal information must remain subject to appropriate privacy protections and required notice.

We do not permit the sale or rental of personal information or its use for targeted advertising. External platforms handle information under their own policies, including Discord and, for email support, Google.

Service providers may process information outside your country. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we will use the required safeguards. Contact us for information about providers involved in handling your data.

5. Retention and security

We store some Discord API data outside Discord to operate Nezuko. This data is encrypted at rest and retained for no longer than 30 days. This limit applies to Discord API data wherever it is retained, including in account or gameplay records, operational logs, and backups.

Presence and streaming-activity data and message content received through Discord are not stored outside Discord. These are processed transiently for the enabled features described above.

Support emails you send directly to us are separate from data obtained through Discord’s API. We retain those communications only for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve your request or meet applicable legal obligations. Please do not include copies of Discord messages or sensitive information unless needed to explain your request.

You may request deletion before the retention period ends by emailing nezukoanimesocial@gmail.com. Following verification, we will delete the relevant personal information without undue delay, subject to applicable legal obligations. If a legal obligation prevents deletion of specific information, we will explain the reason where permitted. Leaving one server or removing the bot does not itself submit a deletion request for your cross-server account.

In addition to encryption at rest, we commit to reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, restricted access, and appropriate handling of credentials and service data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Where legally required, we will notify affected users and relevant parties of a personal-data breach.

6. Your choices and requests

Users can opt out of presence tracking and message-content tracking. Opting out may make streaming-role automation, text commands, quizzes, or other features that rely on that processing unavailable to you. For help exercising an opt-out, email nezukoanimesocial@gmail.com and identify which tracking you want to opt out of.

You can stop using Nezuko at any time. Server administrators can restrict the bot’s permissions or remove it from their servers. To request access, correction, a copy, or deletion of your personal information, email nezukoanimesocial@gmail.com with the subject “Nezuko privacy request,” your Discord user ID, and a description of the request.

We may ask you to demonstrate control of the relevant account before releasing or deleting information. Do not send your password or authentication token. We aim to respond within one month and will follow any shorter legally required deadline. If a lawful extension is necessary, we will explain why and when you can expect a response.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, receive portable information, withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing, and complain to your local data protection authority. Deleting game-account information may permanently remove your collection and progress. We cannot delete messages or copies independently controlled by Discord or other users.

7. Legal bases and age eligibility

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to provide the service you request, for legitimate interests in operating and securing Nezuko and responding to support requests, to comply with legal obligations, or with consent where required. Legitimate interests do not override your fundamental rights. Contact us to ask about the basis applicable to your information or to object.

Nezuko is intended for people who meet Discord’s minimum-age requirements in their country and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe an ineligible child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

8. This policy website

This static page contains no analytics or advertising scripts, forms, cookies, or browser-storage code. GitHub Pages hosts the page and may process technical request information, such as your IP address, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Linked services have their own privacy practices.

9. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when the service or its data practices change. The date shown on this page identifies the latest version. For material changes, we will provide an appropriate notice through the bot or its community channels where required. Where a change requires consent, we will obtain it before applying that change.
