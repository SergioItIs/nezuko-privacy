Privacy Policy

How information relates to Nezuko’s character collections, games, profiles, and Discord interactions.

Draft for operator review. This document is not yet an effective privacy notice. The operator must confirm the data practices, complete the marked details, and approve the policy before it is submitted to Discord or presented as the application’s final policy.

1. About this policy

Nezuko is a Discord entertainment and social gaming bot offering character cards, gacha, missions, battles, social interactions, customizable profiles, and cross-server progression. This policy is intended to describe information processed when people use the bot and contact its support team.

To complete: the operator’s legal name, location, privacy contact, and policy effective date.

2. Information used to provide Nezuko

The following categories reflect Nezuko’s described features and must be checked against the bot’s actual implementation:

  • Discord account information, such as user IDs, usernames, display names, and avatars, to associate gameplay with an account and display profiles.
  • Server and channel identifiers and bot configuration, to deliver commands and gameplay in the appropriate community and channel.
  • Gameplay information, such as collected cards, Moon Fragments (MON), character affection, progression, rewards, profile customization, and competition participation.
  • Command inputs and interactions needed to respond to requests, including slash commands and supported n. prefix commands.
  • Information voluntarily supplied in support requests, such as issue descriptions and diagnostic context.

These categories support command responses, saved collections, cross-server progression, social gameplay, and troubleshooting. Exact logging, analytics, payment-related processing, and any additional purposes must be disclosed if used.

3. Discord permissions and intents

Discord permissions and gateway intents determine which events and information a bot can receive. Access to an event does not, by itself, establish whether the information is stored.

Message Content: Supported text commands such as n.hug require the bot to interpret command text. The operator must confirm whether other message content is processed, whether attachments are accessed, and whether messages are logged or retained.

Server Members: The operator must identify the features that require member information or membership updates and explain which fields are processed and retained.

Presence: Presence events can contain online status and activity information. The operator must confirm the actual feature requiring this access, the information used, and whether it is retained. No presence-based feature has been established for this draft.

4. Visibility and sharing

Commands and gameplay responses posted in Discord channels can be visible to people with access to those channels. Profiles, character showcases, and multiplayer activities may reveal the information users choose to display. Nezuko’s described cross-server features allow collections and progression to be used in more than one server.

Discord processes information under its own Privacy Policy.

To complete: hosting and database providers, analytics or other processors, data locations and international transfers, any legal disclosures, and whether information is sold, shared for advertising, or used for AI training. Do not state that these activities do not occur until verified.

5. Retention and security

Saved collections and progression require information to persist between sessions. Leaving one server is not necessarily a request to delete a cross-server game account.

To complete: retention periods or decision criteria for gameplay records, command logs, support requests, and backups; deletion procedures and timing; and security measures actually in place.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You can stop interacting with the bot. Server administrators can control its access to their server through Discord permissions or remove it. These actions do not establish that previously saved records have been deleted.

Depending on the law applicable to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing of, or receive a copy of personal information. Where processing relies on consent, you may have a right to withdraw it. You may also have a right to complain to a relevant data protection authority.

To complete: a working privacy email or support link, instructions for access and deletion requests, identity verification, and response procedures. Confirm the legal basis for each processing purpose where required. The existing /report issue-reporting command has not been verified as a privacy-request channel.

Nezuko’s age eligibility and handling of children’s information must also be confirmed and aligned with applicable requirements and Discord’s minimum-age rules.

7. This policy website

This static page contains no analytics scripts, advertising scripts, forms, cookies, or browser storage code. When published on GitHub Pages, GitHub’s handling of website traffic is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This statement describes the page’s code, not the bot’s separate data practices.

8. Changes to this policy

The final policy should display its effective date and explain how users will be notified of material changes. Changes to the bot’s data practices should be reflected in the published policy.
